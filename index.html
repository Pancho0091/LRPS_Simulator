02Variables and their sensitivity
VariableEffect on dopeSensitivity
RangeDrop grows faster than linearly: the bullet slows down, so it spends more time falling each yard.Dominant
Muzzle velocityFaster = less time of flight = less drop and less drift.High. ±25 fps ≈ ±0.1–0.2 mil at 800+ yd
BCHigher BC = retains velocity = flatter at long range. Barely matters inside 300 yd.Grows with range
Density altitudeThinner air (hot, high, low pressure) = less drag = less drop.Medium. ~0.15 mil per 1000 ft DA at 1000 yd
WindDrift is roughly linear in crosswind speed: 5 mph is half of 10 mph.Largest source of misses
Shot angleGravity only acts across the line of sight with factor cos(angle), so you need less elevation going up OR down.Small below ~15°
03Conversion rules (the arithmetic on the card) 
1 MIL  at Y yards = 3.6   × (Y / 100) inches
1 MOA  at Y yards = 1.047 × (Y / 100) inches

correction_in_MIL = inches / (3.6   × Y/100)
correction_in_MOA = inches / (1.047 × Y/100)

clicks = round(correction / click_size)        // 0.1 MIL or 0.25 MOA typical

wind_for_speed S = wind_value_10mph × (S / 10)  // linear scaling
wind_for_clock C = full_value × sin(C × 30°)    // 3 & 9 = full, 12 & 6 = zero
  1:30 / 4:30 ≈ 0.7 value     1 / 2 o'clock ≈ 0.5 / 0.87 value
04Exceptions: where the simple rules break
ExceptionCauseWhat to do
MOA vs "shooter's MOA" (IPHY)True MOA is 1.047 in/100 yd; some scopes use 1.000.Know which your turret is. At 1000 yd the difference is ~1.4 in per MOA dialed.
Transonic zoneNear Mach 1 (~1125 fps) the drag curve spikes and the bullet can destabilise. Solver accuracy degrades.Mark the range where velocity < ~1340 fps (Mach 1.2) on the card. Trust field data past it.
Solver ≠ realityChronograph error, BC that differs from the box, scope tracking error."True" the card: shoot at long range, adjust MV (or BC) until predictions match impacts.
Wind is not one numberWind at the muzzle, midrange and target differ; gusts.The card gives you brackets (5/10/15 mph). Your wind call picks the bracket.
Spin driftGyroscopic effect pushes right-twist bullets right, growing with time of flight.Small (<0.2 mil inside 800 yd). Some shooters fold it into the card's wind zero.
Card made for one DADrop changes with air density.Make one card per typical DA band (e.g. 0 / 3000 / 6000 ft), or recompute on the day.
05Code analog 
// The card is a memoised function with a coarse key and interpolation.
const card = precompute(solver, ranges = [100, 150, ... 1000], conditions = today);
function dope(range, windMph, clock) {
  const elev = interpolate(card.elev, range);                 // between rows
  const wind = interpolate(card.wind10, range) * windMph/10 * sin(clock*30°);
  return { elev: roundToClick(elev), wind: roundToClick(wind) };
}
